Ball motion

Rigid-body physics: Coulomb friction at the contact point (oil ≈ 0.04, dry ≈ 0.20), Euler rigid-body rotation using the drilled ball's RG / differential / intermediate differential oriented by your layout, so track flare and axis migration come out of the simulation. Also modeled: oil picked up and absorbed by the cover, oil thinning down the pattern, how the layout changes the drilled RG and differentials, the drilling angle + VAL angle timing (sum and ratio), and the rev-up where the axis crosses the pin-to-spin line. Not modeled: lane topography, CG offset, pin action. Use it to compare layouts; confirm on the lanes.

Fit specs — inches, 1/64ths

Laid out like a pro shop grip card, looking at the ball with the finger holes up. Type each pitch in the box on the side it points to (one per pair), and the hole size inside each circle. Each span box has a dropdown: Cut to cut (thumb-hole edge to finger-hole edge, measured to the drilled cut — the layout math uses this one) or Full span (with the inserts in, what the hand feels). Both are kept; the dropdown picks which one you're editing and which one prints. Insert style and size: pick from the list, or choose Other… to type your own. Saved with the bowler (press Save in the Bowler card). Fractions are rounded to the nearest 1/64".

Spare system — straight shots over the arrows

Stand board = the foul-line board for your target arrow + laydown distance, adjusted for drift. Pick the arrow you like for each pin; saved with the bowler. Arrows are placed at their usual spots (center arrow ~15½ ft out).
